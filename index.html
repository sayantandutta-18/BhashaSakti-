DigitalOcean • Best Open-Source AI Project

100% Open-Weight End-to-End Pipeline

Every core stage of BhashaSandhi is powered by open-source or open-weight AI models:

  • STT: OpenAI Whisper large-v3 (1.55B params, MIT license) running on cloud / local faster-whisper.
  • TTS: Meta MMS-TTS (VITS, CC-BY-NC 4.0) with native acoustic models for Hindi, Bengali, and Odia.
  • Harness: Custom Flask model harness containerized with PyTorch on DigitalOcean App Platform.
  • License: Full OSI-approved MIT License at repository root.
Google • Best Use of Gemma 4

Multimodal Vision & Indic Language Reasoning

Powered by Google's Gemma 4 (gemma-4-26b-a4b-it / gemma-4-31b-it) accessed through the Gemini API:

  • Multimodal Experience: Upload or snap photographs; Gemma 4 analyzes pixel inputs and explains visual content in spoken Indic scripts.
  • Focused AI Tool: Tool grounding connecting live Open-Meteo weather and Asia/Kolkata IST time directly to spoken sentences.
  • Rapid Prototyping: Dynamic model discovery, streaming token generation via Server-Sent Events, and thought filtering.
